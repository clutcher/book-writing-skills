epub3-template.html and epub3-template.pandoc-default.html

Both files come from pandoc's default epub3 template (pandoc 3.8.3,
https://github.com/jgm/pandoc, data/templates/default.epub3).
epub3-template.pandoc-default.html is an unchanged copy; epub3-template.html
carries three small changes, described in references/epub.md.
