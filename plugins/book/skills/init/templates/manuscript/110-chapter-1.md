# Chapter 1: The First Chapter

Open the chapter with the question it answers, so the reader knows why the pages that follow matter. Develop the answer step by step, and give each step the reasoning and the evidence behind it. End the chapter with the conclusion the reader could almost reach alone from what came before.

## Summary

Sum up the chapter in a few sentences that repeat its main idea in plain words. Point to the next chapter and name the question it will answer for the reader. Keep the summary short, because its job is to help the reader remember and not to add new ideas.
