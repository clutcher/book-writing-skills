# Intended impression

What a stranger should believe after the thumbnail, the store description and the back cover, and by what means. The prepare-sales-text skill's impression review compares what is received with this file; a gap in either direction is a finding.

## What the surfaces must produce

- **The book:** <kind of book; read once or kept>
- **The author:** <standing, and how it is shown, never claimed>
- **The ideas:** <own position or synthesis; the thesis a reader can repeat>
- **The authority:** <what it rests on>
- **The seriousness:** <what signals years of work>
- **The category:** <the one shelf>

## By what means, and what is ruled out

- Allowed: <precision, structure, a claim the reader can test, ...>
- Ruled out: <credentials as authority, superlatives, borrowed names, ...>
