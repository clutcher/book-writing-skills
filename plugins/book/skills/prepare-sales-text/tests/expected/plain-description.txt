Why does one loaf rise high while the next one sinks in the oven?

A starter fed at the wrong hour and a dough rushed to the oven explain most of the loaves that fail at home. Two decades of bakery notes show where the time goes.

This book follows the dough through three stages, from the first mix to the oven door.

What is inside

- The Starter. Feeding and keeping a starter.
- The Bakery. The same method at bakery size.

Recipes use rye and wheat.
