# Evidence grades

A grade says **where a measurement was taken**, not how much anyone likes the idea. Use it in
Stage 2 (Evidence Sufficiency, Reasoning Chain Validation) and in the verification pass to decide
what a sentence may state. A weak grade is not a reason to drop a claim; it is a reason to write
the claim as what it is.

The scale fits any book that rests claims on measurements, whether from its own field or from
another one. "The book's domain" is the subject the book teaches; "a nearby domain" is the closest
measured setting (for a book about training runners: studies of cyclists).

| Grade | Meaning | What the book may say |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Measured in the book's domain, with a manipulation | State it as a finding, with its numbers |
| **B** | Measured in a nearby domain | State it as a finding about that domain, then argue the transfer in so many words |
| **C** | Measured in a distant domain | Use it only as an illustration, never as the step the argument rests on |
| **D** | Asserted by a review; the primary study is not held | Attribute it to the review and say the primary was not read |
| **E** | A premise a research tradition assumed, not a result it produced | Never quote it as evidence: it went in, so it cannot come out |
| **F** | An argument by analogy; no measurement of this link exists | Say "this is an argument" in those words |
| **X** | Contradicted, or dead: better-scoped work runs against it | Do not use. Record it so it is not rediscovered |

Two flags sit beside the grade, because a grade alone hides them:

- **[small]**: the effect exists and explains little of what varies.
- **[single]**: one study, not replicated.

## Using the grades on a chain

Grade every link of a load-bearing chain separately, then name the weakest link. The chain is
only as strong as that link, whatever the other grades are. A useful last question for a
chain that rests on a mechanism: does the advice still stand if the mechanism is refuted? If it
does, the book can say so, and the chain's weakest link stops being a risk to the advice.
