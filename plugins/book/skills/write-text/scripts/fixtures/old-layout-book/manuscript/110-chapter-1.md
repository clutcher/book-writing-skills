# Chapter 1: What Rising Means

## 1.1 What A Dough Holds

The lean dough in this chapter rises slowly, which means a baker can sleep through the whole night without checking it. Rich dough does the opposite. The lean crust of the loaf grows.

> **A poke test** is a quick press of the dough that shows whether it has risen enough.

## Summary

The lean dough of this chapter rises without help, which is the point every later chapter builds on.
