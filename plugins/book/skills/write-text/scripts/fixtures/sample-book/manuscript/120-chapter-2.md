# Chapter 2: What Folding Means

## 2.1 What A Fold Costs

Nkem Okafor, a baker who studied how flour takes up water, showed that a wet dough needs about four folds before it holds its shape [Oka21]. Each fold takes the same few minutes, so the “cost” of a wet dough grows with every fold a baker must make — and the baker feels it.

The lean dough of Chapter 1 needs fewer folds, which means a baker can leave it alone for most of the evening without checking on it every single hour.
