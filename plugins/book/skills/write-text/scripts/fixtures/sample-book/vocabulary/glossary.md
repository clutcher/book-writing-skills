# Glossary

## Defined terms

### float test -- Ch1 S1.1 (blockquote)

**Canonical definition:** a check that drops a spoon of starter into water to see whether it floats.

### lean dough -- Ch1 S1.2 (definition sentence)

**Canonical definition:** dough that rises at one slow, even pace.

### dough -- rich -- Chapter 1 (definition sentence)

**Canonical definition:** dough that rises too fast and falls back.

## Usage and drift guards

### habit (word discipline) -- book usage

**Canonical definition:** kept for the two habits the book teaches.
